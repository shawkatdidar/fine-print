RETAIL LEASE

This Retail Lease (this "Lease") is entered into as of the Effective Date by and between Ashgrove Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and the party identified as Tenant on the signature page ("Tenant"). Landlord and Tenant are each a "Party" and together the "Parties". This is a fictional document created for a demonstration; all names, places and figures are invented.

WHEREAS, Landlord owns the retail center known as Calder Street Market Hall; WHEREAS, Tenant wishes to lease space in it for the operation of a café; NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the rent to be paid and the covenants set out below, the Parties agree as follows.

ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 Definitions. In this Lease, the following terms have the meanings given below.

"Additional Rent" means all amounts other than Base Rent payable by Tenant under this Lease.

"Base Rent" means the monthly rent set out in Exhibit B.

"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday in the City.

"Common Areas" means the walkways, parking areas, landscaping and other areas of the Project available for the common use of tenants.

"Commencement Date" means the date determined under Section 4.2.

"Lease Year" means each period of twelve (12) consecutive full calendar months beginning on the Commencement Date.

"Operating Expenses" means the costs of operating, maintaining and insuring the Project, subject to the exclusions in Exhibit G.

"Permitted Use" means the use described in Section 8.1.

"Premises" means the space known as Unit 4, Calder Street Market Hall, shown on Exhibit C.

"Project" means the land described in Exhibit A and the buildings and improvements on it.

"Proportionate Share" means the percentage stated in Section 6.3.

"Security Deposit" means the amount described in Article 7.

"Term" means the period described in Article 4, including any renewal.

"Alteration" means any alteration, addition or improvement to the Premises.

"Hazardous Material" means any substance regulated as hazardous or toxic under applicable environmental law.

"Guarantor" means each person who signs the Guaranty attached as Exhibit F.

"Event of Default" means any of the events described in Section 18.1.

1.2 Interpretation. Notwithstanding Section 1.1, this Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter. No amendment to this Lease shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both Parties. This Lease may be executed in counterparts. Except as expressly set out in this Lease, no waiver shall be effective unless in writing.
